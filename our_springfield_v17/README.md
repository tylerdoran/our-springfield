Our Springfield v1.7 — Land Expansion

Extract this ZIP and upload index.html to the root of your GitHub Pages repository, replacing the previous index.html. Keep your existing manifest, icons and other files.

NEW: 36 by 36 tile map divided into 81 purchasable 4 by 4 plots. Nine plots are owned at the start of a new town. Tap the 🗺️ button to buy neighboring plots using unlimited cash or donuts. Locked plots are tinted; buildings cannot be placed on locked land. Land ownership is saved on this device. Existing buildings and story progress are preserved; existing buildings outside the starting zone have their plot automatically preserved.

This update does not reset your town. Other Tapped Out content, events, buildings, quests and multiplayer remain future development, not part of this update.
